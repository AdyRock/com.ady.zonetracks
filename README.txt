ZoneTracks turns your Homey into a smart family locator by using OwnTracks on your mobile phone to report real-time location updates.
It helps you see where your loved ones are, automate home routines based on arrival and departure, and get better visibility into daily movement.
Perfect for smarter arrivals, home presence automation, and a more connected household.

Map tiles: When you view a map, ZoneTracks requests the required map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's community tile service. These requests disclose the approximate map area being viewed and normal network information, such as the Homey connection's IP address and the ZoneTracks application identifier. ZoneTracks does not send names, stored journeys, zone names, avatars, or raw OwnTracks location reports to OpenStreetMap. See the [OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
